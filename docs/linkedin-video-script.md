# LinkedIn video script: shopify-black-friday

**Length:** 60–90 s · **Audience:** fashion brand owners and e-commerce
managers on Shopify, not developers · **Presenter:** Karina (voice-over or
on camera) · **Format:** vertical 4:5 or 9:16, burned-in captions (most people
watch muted).

| # | Time | Visual | On-screen caption | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3 s | Karina to camera, or a "−30% EVERYTHING" banner being crossed out | **Stop discounting your bestsellers this Black Friday.** | "Stop discounting your bestsellers this Black Friday." |
| 2 | 3–12 s | A product card: €89 dress, "−30%", then the price breaking down: VAT, returns, fees | 30% off can mean zero profit | "Thirty percent off sounds safe. But take out VAT, payment fees, shipping, and the one in three orders that come back, and many 'deals' make nothing at all." |
| 3 | 12–20 s | Claude chat: "Is 30% off everything a good idea?" | Ask in plain English | "So we built a free assistant for Shopify fashion brands. You ask your Black Friday question, and it asks you the right ones back." |
| 4 | 20–30 s | The interview: "Rank these: profit, clearing stock, new customers…" | It asks before it advises | "Your costs, your returns, your VAT, what you need to clear, and what matters most to you this year." |
| 5 | 30–42 s | The table: "Max safe discount 20% · you keep €19.55 per sale · needs +49% sales" | Every product's safe discount, in euros | "Then, for every product: the deepest discount that still makes money, what you keep per sale, and how many more sales it needs to be worth it." |
| 6 | 42–52 s | Protect / Hero / Clearance labels on products | Protect bestsellers · discount the right items · clear old stock | "New arrivals and bestsellers stay full price. A few hero deals bring traffic. Old stock is priced to move, without selling at a loss unless you choose to." |
| 7 | 52–62 s | Three scenario cards; "Recommended" badge on one | Three options, one clear recommendation | "You get three options, from cautious to bold, and a clear recommendation based on your goal, not ours." |
| 8 | 62–72 s | Plan table → "Yes" → "5 discounts scheduled" | Nothing changes until you say yes | "When you agree, it sets up the discounts in Shopify, with end dates, and everything can be undone." |
| 9 | 72–80 s | EU flag with "30-day lowest price" | EU price rules flagged (info, not legal advice) | "Selling in Europe? It flags the 30-day price rules before you advertise a percentage." |
| 10 | 80–90 s | GitHub repo page, SIXFIT.AI logo | **Free & open source → github.com/sixfit-ai/shopify-black-friday** | "It's free and open source. Link in the comments. Price smart this Black Friday." |

**Post copy (suggestion):**
> Most fashion brands lose margin on Black Friday by discounting the wrong
> products, too deeply. We built a free Claude skill for Shopify that works out
> each product's safe discount after VAT, fees and returns, and recommends the
> right offer for your goal. Link in the first comment.

**Notes for editing:**
- Keep every caption under ~8 words, high-contrast, in the bottom third.
- Use the test store for all screen footage. Blur any real customer data.
- The numbers in scenes 2 and 5 come from the offline test fixture, not a real
  store. Re-record from your test store if you want real figures.
- Don't promise results ("+30% revenue"). The skill makes no such claim.
